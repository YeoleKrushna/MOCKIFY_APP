Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Mockify (“Mockify”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides AI-generated mock tests and related learning features through mockify.tech. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how we share it with service providers, and the choices available to you.

Please read this policy together with our Terms of Service. By creating an account or using Mockify, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account and contact information

When you register through email, we may collect your name and email address. Email verification and password-related security data may also be stored to operate your account securely.

Google sign-in information

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google may provide information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address and Google account identifier, as permitted by the authorization flow. We use this information to create or authenticate your Mockify account and do not receive your Google password.

Mock-test and learning data

We store the topics you submit, generated questions, your answers, scores, time taken, result history and related learning information so that Mockify can generate tests, display results and maintain your history.

Feedback and support information

If you submit feedback or a bug report, we may collect the message, category and any attachment you choose to provide.

Technical and usage information

We may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, session information, basic request logs and service-health or usage events. Some analytics information may be used in aggregated form to understand product usage and reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your Mockify account and authenticate you.
  • To send verification, account and service-related emails.
  • To generate mock tests, calculate scores and show explanations and history.
  • To provide support, troubleshoot bugs and respond to feedback.
  • To protect the service against abuse, fraud, unauthorized access and security threats.
  • To understand product performance, improve reliability and develop features.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. AI-Generated Content

Mockify uses third-party AI infrastructure to generate questions and educational explanations. Information needed to produce the requested output may be transmitted to the AI service provider. Do not enter sensitive personal information into a topic prompt or feedback message unless it is necessary for your request.

4. Service Providers

We use third-party infrastructure providers to operate Mockify. Depending on the feature, these may include Google for authentication, Brevo for transactional email delivery, Groq for AI generation, Neon for database hosting, Render for application hosting, and Cloudflare for DNS and related infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

5. Cookies, Sessions and Similar Technologies

Mockify uses browser storage and server sessions as necessary to keep you signed in, protect authenticated actions and remember limited application state. We may also use standard technical mechanisms required to operate the website securely and reliably.
